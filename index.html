What's inside

Built for the people behind the lens, the brush, and the beat.

Every feature was designed for working creatives — not influencers, not brands, not noise.

Community feed

Share photos, video and audio with the community. Multi-image galleries, reactions beyond a like.

Voice messages

When typing isn't enough — record and send voice notes in any conversation, solo or group.

Collab requests

Send collaboration invites with a personal note and an attached project. Free members get 5 a month.

Direct messages

Private conversations with your network — one to one, or with your whole inner circle at once.

Inner circle

Curate your closest collaborators. Group chats, shared moodboards, your most trusted people in one place.

Projects & moodboards

Plan shoots and releases, invite collaborators, and build moodboards that align everyone on one aesthetic.

Discover

Find creatives by role, location and availability — photographers, stylists, directors, and a hundred more.

Smart notifications

Granular controls — choose exactly what reaches you: connections, collabs, messages, reactions.

Profile viewers Pro

See who's been checking out your work — with analytics on views, connections, and growth.
