A platform with a point of view

Where creative professionals find their people.

Tribes is a social platform built for the creative industry — photographers, directors, stylists, musicians, designers and the dozens of disciplines in between. Discover collaborators. Share your work. Build your inner circle.

Manifesto

Built for the people behind the lens, the brush, and the beat.

Every feature in Tribes was designed with creative professionals in mind — not influencers, not brands, not noise. Just the people who make the work, finding the people who help them make it better.

What's inside

Nine ways to find your tribe.

01

Community Feed

Share your creative work with the world. Photos, video, audio. Swipe through multi-image galleries and react with more than just a like.

02

Video Calls

Connect face-to-face with collaborators in HD video calls — built directly into the app. No third-party tools, no friction.

03

Collab Requests

Send and receive collaboration invites with a personal note. Build creative partnerships with people who match your vision.

04

Direct Messaging

Private conversations with your network. Message individuals or your entire inner circle at once. Everything in one place.

05

Inner Circle

Curate your closest collaborators into a private inner circle. Share moodboards, start group chats, keep your most trusted network close.

06

Projects & Moodboards

Create and manage creative projects, invite collaborators, and build visual moodboards to align your team around a shared aesthetic.

07

Discover

Find talented creatives by role, location, and availability. Filter by photographer, stylist, director, makeup artist — and hundreds more.

08

Smart Notifications

Granular notification controls. Choose exactly what matters — connections, collabs, messages, reactions — and silence the rest.

09

Creative Profiles

Build a rich creative profile that showcases your roles, portfolio, bio, and availability. Let the right people find you.

A first look

Designed in the dark, for the people who light it.

Tribes is a dark-first experience — built so your work, not the chrome around it, takes the stage. Cinematic, quiet, considered.

  • Editorial dark theme that lets photography and video breathe
  • One-tap collab requests, right from someone's profile
  • Built-in HD video calls for casting, briefs and creative direction
  • Inner Circle group chats for your tightest collaborators
