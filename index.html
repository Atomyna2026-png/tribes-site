Where creative professionals find their people

Tribes is a social platform built for the creative industry — photographers, directors, stylists, musicians, designers and more. Discover collaborators, share your work, and build your inner circle.

What's inside

📸 01

Community Feed

Share your creative work with the world. Post photos, videos, and audio. Swipe through multi-image galleries and react with more than just a like.

🎬 02

Video Calls

Connect face-to-face with collaborators in HD video calls — built directly into the app. No third-party apps needed.

✨ 03

Collab Requests

Send and receive collaboration requests with a personal message. Build creative partnerships with professionals who match your vision.

💬 04

Direct Messaging

Private conversations with your network. Message individuals or your entire inner circle at once. Everything in one place.

⭕ 05

Inner Circle

Curate your closest collaborators into a private inner circle. Share moodboards, start group chats, and keep your most trusted network close.

🗂 06

Projects & Moodboards

Create and manage creative projects, invite collaborators, and build visual moodboards to align your team around a shared aesthetic.

🔍 07

Discover

Find talented creatives by role, location, and availability. Filter by photographer, stylist, director, makeup artist — and hundreds more.

🔔 08

Smart Notifications

Stay in the loop with granular notification controls. Choose exactly what matters — connections, collabs, messages, reactions, and more.

🌐 09

Creative Profiles

Build a rich creative profile that showcases your roles, portfolio, bio, and availability. Let the right people find you.
