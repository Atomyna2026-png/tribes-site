Privacy Policy

Tribes: Private Communities · Effective August 9, 2026

Tribes ("we," "us") is a private community app for creatives. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Tribes iOS app and the tribes.network website.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information — email address, display name, username, password (stored by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text), profile photo, bio, creative roles, and profile details you choose to add.
  • Content you create — posts, comments, direct messages, group chats, voice messages, moodboards, projects, collaboration requests, and images you upload.
  • Location (optional) — an approximate location you provide for your profile, used to show nearby collaborators in Discover. The app requests location only while in use, and you can decline.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage and device information — basic app interaction data, device tokens for push notifications, and diagnostic information provided by our infrastructure providers.
  • Profile views — when you view another member's profile, that view is recorded so members with a Pro subscription can see who viewed their profile.

Purchase information: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive subscription status (active, expired, refunded) from Apple to unlock Pro features, along with an anonymous transaction identifier. We never receive your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app: profiles, messaging, communities, moodboards, projects, and collaboration features.
  • To deliver push notifications you've enabled (messages, likes, comments, collaboration requests). You can turn these off in Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.
  • To operate Pro subscription features, including profile-viewer lists and analytics.
  • To keep the community safe: processing reports, enforcing blocks, and moderating content that violates our Terms of Use.
  • To maintain, debug, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. What Other Members Can See

Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, roles, approximate location if provided, and public activity) is visible to other signed-in members. Direct messages are visible only to conversation participants. Private projects and moodboards are visible only to you and collaborators you add. If you subscribe to Pro, a verified badge is displayed on your profile.

4. Service Providers

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run Tribes. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, and push notification delivery. Firebase Privacy & Security →
  • Apple — push notification delivery (APNs) and subscription billing (App Store). Apple Privacy Policy →

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account and personal data from our systems; some content may persist briefly in backups before being purged, and we may retain limited records where required by law (for example, records related to safety reports).

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (editing your profile, deleting content, deleting your account). For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children

Tribes is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access to member data is restricted by server-side security rules, and privileged operations (such as subscription status) are handled only by our servers, never by the client app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the app. The effective date at the top reflects the latest revision, and continued use of Tribes after a change constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: support@tribes.network
